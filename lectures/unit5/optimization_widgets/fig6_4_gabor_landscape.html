Points labeled b–f are the ones the book shows as insets. The unlabeled cyan circles are other local minima. At every minimum no small change of φ lowers the loss, yet only c is the best fit. At the saddle point, the loss falls if you move φ₀ either way but rises if you move φ₁.

The least-squares loss is L[φ] = Σᵢ (f[xᵢ, φ] − yᵢ)², summed over the training data (orange). The data were generated from a Gabor model with φ = [0, 16.6] plus noise. φ₀ moves the function left and right, and φ₁ squeezes it along the x-axis.
