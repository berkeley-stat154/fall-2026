Shallow network terminology

Fully connected, feed-forward network with one hidden layer of four hidden units

Each arrow is a weight (a slope parameter). The biases (offset parameters) are not drawn. Each hidden unit computes a pre-activation (a weighted sum of its inputs plus a bias), then applies the ReLU to get its activation hd.
